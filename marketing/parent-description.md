# CRUNCHER — for parents

CRUNCHER is a fast, loud, neon arcade game for Meta Quest 3 where your kid plays a
glitch gremlin punching their way through a corrupted computer. Every round flashes a
rule — "MULTIPLES OF 7," "MAKE 24," "FACTORS OF 36" — and they smash the number blocks
that fit while goofy creatures like a router-puppy and a sock-crocodile get in the way.
It looks and feels like a game, not homework. Underneath, a spaced-repetition engine
tracks every multiplication fact from 2×2 to 12×12 and quietly brings back the ones your
kid misses or answers slowly, so they get extra practice exactly where they need it.
There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts and no chat, and nothing ever leaves the
headset: progress is saved only on the device. Open **STATS → BRAIN.EXE** any time to see
a heat map of which facts they've locked in.
